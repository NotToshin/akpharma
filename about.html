Our Company

Advancing Healthcare,
One Partnership at a Time

Since 2014, Meridian Pharma has been committed to improving access to quality pharmaceutical products for patients and physicians across Bangladesh.

Our Mission

To Make Quality Medicine Accessible

Our mission is to close the gap between globally validated pharmaceutical innovation and the patients of Bangladesh who stand to benefit from it. We do this by carefully selecting products with proven clinical efficacy, partnering with trustworthy international manufacturers, and deploying a knowledgeable field force that educates physicians with integrity.

Our Vision

Bangladesh's Most Trusted Pharma Marketer

By 2030, Meridian Pharma aspires to be recognized as the most trusted pharmaceutical marketing company in Bangladesh — known not merely for the products we sell, but for the standards we set, the physicians we empower, and the patients whose lives we positively impact.

Company History

A Decade of Deliberate Growth

From a small startup to a nationally recognized pharmaceutical marketer — our journey has been defined by disciplined decision-making, meaningful partnerships, and an uncompromising commitment to quality.

View Our Products
2014
Meridian Pharma Founded
Company established in Dhaka with a core team of three, focusing initially on maternal health supplements sourced from South Korea and Europe.
2016
DGDA License & First Import
Received full DGDA import authorization. First consignment of 12 products landed — distributed across Dhaka, Chattogram, and Sylhet.
2018
Medical Representative Team Expanded
Grew field force to 10 dedicated Medical Representatives, enabling coverage of all 8 administrative divisions of Bangladesh.
2020
Sexual Wellness Portfolio Launch
Introduced a comprehensive reproductive health and sexual wellness product line — a first for a dedicated pharma marketer in Bangladesh.
2022
3,000+ Physician Milestone
Surpassed 3,000 active prescribing physician relationships. ISO-compliant central warehouse inaugurated in Gazipur.
2024
10-Year Anniversary
Celebrating a decade of commitment to Bangladeshi healthcare with 40+ SKUs, expanded logistics infrastructure, and continued growth.
What We Stand For

Principles That Guide Every Decision

Our values are not aspirational statements. They are operational standards embedded into every interaction, every product choice, and every relationship we build.

Clinical Integrity

We only represent products supported by robust clinical evidence. Our Medical Representatives are trained to communicate product information accurately, without exaggeration or omission.

Patient First

Every product decision, pricing model, and distribution strategy is evaluated through one lens: will this benefit the patient? We never sacrifice patient welfare for commercial gain.

Partnership

Physicians are our partners, not mere customers. We invest in long-term professional relationships built on mutual respect, reliable service, and shared commitment to better health outcomes.

Regulatory Trust

Compliance is not a checkbox — it is a culture. We maintain impeccable regulatory standards, work transparently with the DGDA, and hold our supply chain partners to the same expectations.

Continuous Improvement

The pharmaceutical landscape evolves rapidly. We actively seek new clinical developments, emerging therapeutic areas, and innovative delivery formats to keep our portfolio at the frontier of care.

Reliability

When a physician writes a prescription, the product must be available. Our logistics and inventory management systems are engineered for consistent, on-time delivery with zero tolerance for stockouts.

Compliance & Quality

Regulatory Standards We Uphold

Every product we import, store, and distribute complies with national and international pharmaceutical regulations.

DGDA Licensed

WHO GMP Compliant

Cold Chain Certified

Batch Traceability

Complete documentation chain from manufacturer batch release to end-point pharmacy dispensing.
