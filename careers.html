Who We're Looking For

The Ideal Medical Representative Profile

We recruit professionals who combine scientific curiosity with commercial drive and genuine care for improving patient outcomes.

Educational Background

B.Sc in Life Sciences, Pharmacy, or a health-related discipline preferred. Equivalent experience considered.

Communication Skills

Confident verbal communicator in both Bangla and English. Ability to explain complex clinical concepts simply and persuasively.

Territory Knowledge

Familiarity with the medical community in your target division. Existing physician relationships are a strong advantage.

Ethical Conduct

Non-negotiable. We operate with full transparency and expect every representative to uphold the highest professional and ethical standards.

Self-Motivation & Mobility

Comfortable working independently in the field, managing your own schedule, and meeting call coverage and prescription targets each cycle.

Our Hiring Process

How We Hire

We keep our hiring process transparent, respectful, and efficient. From application to offer typically takes 2–3 weeks.

1
Submit Your Application

Complete the online application form below with your CV and a short cover statement explaining your interest in pharmaceutical marketing.

2
Initial Screening Call

A 20-minute call with our HR team to discuss your background, experience, and the role expectations. Conducted by phone or video.

3
Field & Product Interview

An in-person interview with the Sales Manager including a brief product detailing exercise to assess communication and clinical comprehension.

4
Offer & Onboarding

Successful candidates receive a formal offer letter followed by a structured 2-week onboarding program covering products, protocols, and territory orientation.
