Who We Are

Your Trusted Companion in Health & Specialized Wellness

Founded in 2014, AK Pharma is a Dhaka-based pharmaceutical marketing and distribution leader. We procure clinically validated medicinal formulations in bulk from top-tier international manufacturers, marketing them directly to physicians and delivering them seamlessly to model pharmacies and patients nationwide.

Whether you are a mother nurturing new life, an individual seeking balanced vitality, or a physician seeking uncompromised therapeutic efficacy — AK Pharma stands beside you with DGDA-certified quality and compassionate support.

Information & Guidance

Frequently Asked Questions

Key information regarding our regulatory approvals, product authenticity, medical inquiries, and distribution network across Bangladesh.

Yes, 100% of AK Pharma pharmaceutical and nutritional formulations are officially evaluated, licensed, and registered with the Directorate General of Drug Administration (DGDA), Ministry of Health & Family Welfare, Bangladesh. Every imported batch carries full traceability and regulatory compliance documentation.
Registered physicians, specialists, and hospital procurement officers can request product monographs, clinical literature, and physician samples by reaching out via our direct hotline (+880 1798-112763) or through our Contact page. Our dedicated medical representatives will schedule an in-person or virtual consultation.
Our core portfolio is concentrated in specialized therapeutic segments: Maternal & Prenatal Health (such as Ova-Wom and Fol-Aid), Sexual Wellness & Reproductive Health, High-Potency Vitamins & Micronutrients, and specialized Prescription Support formulations meeting WHO-GMP manufacturing standards.
We operate temperature-controlled warehousing and distribution logistics adhering strictly to GSP (Good Storage Practice) guidelines. Sensitive active ingredients are continuously monitored with digital data loggers below 25°C (or 2°C–8°C where required) throughout importation, customs transit, and regional dispatch.
AK Pharma products are supplied across all 8 administrative divisions of Bangladesh through authorized distribution partners, hospital pharmacies, and licensed Model Pharmacies. For immediate stockist locator assistance, reach our direct support desk at +880 1798-112763.
