Who We Are

A Trusted Partner in Bangladesh's Pharmaceutical Landscape

Founded in 2014, AK Pharma Ltd. is a Dhaka-based pharmaceutical marketing company specializing in the importation and distribution of high-quality medicinal products from globally reputable manufacturers.

We operate a dedicated field force of ten experienced Medical Representatives who build lasting professional relationships with physicians across all major cities and districts of Bangladesh.

Quality Assurance

All imports undergo rigorous DGDA-mandated quality checks before distribution.

Physician Network

3,000+ doctors rely on our products for their patients' treatment plans.

Global Sourcing

Strategic supplier relationships across Europe, South Korea, and India.

Rapid Fulfilment

Efficient logistics ensuring timely availability at pharmacies nationwide.
